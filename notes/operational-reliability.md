# Operational Reliability
Many workflow problems are not caused by a single failure, but by small operational inconsistencies accumulating over time.
In AI-assisted systems, workflows may continue running while still producing unreliable outputs.

Operational reliability usually improves when:
* workflows remain observable
* outputs are easy to verify
* automation logic stays understandable
* troubleshooting steps are documented
* unnecessary complexity is reduced

Practical operation is often more important than theoretical optimization.
