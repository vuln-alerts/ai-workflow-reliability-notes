# AI Workflow Reliability Notes
This repository contains practical notes related to AI workflow reliability, troubleshooting, and operational organization.
The focus is on keeping workflows understandable, maintainable, and observable in actual business operation rather than building unnecessarily complex systems.

## Topics
* Workflow reliability
* Operational consistency
* Troubleshooting observations
* Prompt/workflow organization
* AI-assisted workflow issues
* Retrieval and context handling
* Failure visibility
* Workflow maintainability

## Purpose
Many AI-assisted workflows appear to work correctly at first, but become difficult to maintain or troubleshoot over time.

This repository is mainly focused on operational thinking:
* keeping workflows readable
* reducing silent failures
* improving observability
* simplifying troubleshooting
* maintaining workflow consistency

## Notes
The content here is intentionally practical and lightweight.
The goal is not to build overly complex AI systems, but to organize ideas and operational approaches that help AI-assisted workflows remain stable and understandable in real-world use.
